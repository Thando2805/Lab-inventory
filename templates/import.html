How detection works

SOP shape Detected when columns include Inventory ID, Opening Qty, and Min Level. Mapped 1:1 to the Master Inventory.
Snapshot shape Detected when columns include Item Description, Quantity, and Units. Each sheet becomes an Area; categories map to the SOP list (Glassware → Apparatus, Media → Reagent, etc.).
Meta sheets Instructions, Dashboard, Lists, Monthly Count, Discrepancy CAPA and Disposal Register are skipped automatically.
